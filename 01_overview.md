# MPD — Channel & Pipeline Overview

## Channel

- **Handle:** @moneypatternsdecoded
- **Format:** Faceless, voiceover-driven
- **Cadence:** Ship-when-ready, target 3 episodes/week (revised from weekly)
- **Length:** 8–10 min per episode
- **Revenue:** AdSense only (no courses, sponsors, upsells)
- **Launch:** Feb 15, 2026

## Mission

Reveal hidden patterns in money, business, and decisions — exposing the
systems, incentives, and psychology that most people miss. Every video
delivers one clear "Aha!" moment: not just *what* happens, but *why* the
mechanism works the way it does. Educational investigation channel — not
financial advice.

## We are / are not

✓ Educational investigation, pattern recognition, critical thinking,
content-first faceless format, long-term evergreen library.

✗ Get-rich-quick, financial advice, course-selling, personality brand,
entertainment-first.

## 5 Pillars (strict distribution)

| Code | Pillar | Share | Target words |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Why Everyone Gets [X] Wrong | 30% PRIMARY | 1,150–1,300 |
| **P2** | The [X] Explained | 25% SECONDARY | 1,300–1,450 |
| **P3** | The Hidden Cost of [X] | 20% CORE | 1,150–1,300 |
| **P4** | Case Study Narrative | 15% NARRATIVE | 1,350–1,500 |
| **P5** | Psychology of Money | 10% HIGH PERFORMER ↑ | 1,000–1,150 |

**Pillar rule:** never run more than 2 same-pillar videos consecutively.
Mix evergreen + seasonal. No similar topics within 20 videos.

## Audience

- **Primary 60%** — The Curious Professional (25–40, $45K–$120K, knowledge worker)
- **Secondary 30%** — The Ambitious Learner (18–28, building mental models)
- **Tertiary 10%** — The Experienced Skeptic (40–60, $100K+, fresh perspectives)

Universal traits: intellectually curious, skeptical of conventional wisdom,
time-conscious, pattern-oriented, value clarity over complexity.

activates at videos 30–100. Success = consistency over 52 weeks.
